## What this is

**Online Writing** is a collection of AI-agent skills for people who already have ideas, notes, or
opinions and need help turning that raw material into public writing that is easier to follow and
publish. It treats online writing as a system: purpose shapes topics, topics sit inside content
buckets, drafts follow format-specific structures, voice is calibrated from examples, and finished
pieces can be evaluated and reused.

It is **opinionated** and works best with project context (purpose, content buckets, and especially
voice). The skills are inspired by *The Art and Business of Online Writing* and related practice, but
this is an independent collection, not an official companion product.

## The workflow

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="1 · Discover" icon="setting">
		Establish the durable foundation — why you write, which topics you own, and how your

	</Card>
	<Card title="2 · Produce" icon="pencil">
		Mine ideas, explore angles, and shape raw material into short-form posts, long-form
		drafts, or Medium articles. See the [Production](/online-writing-skills/guides/production/) guide.
	</Card>
	<Card title="3 · Refine & reuse" icon="approve-check">
		Improve, diagnose, rate, abstract, and repurpose finished pieces into platform-native
		formats. See the [Review](/online-writing-skills/guides/review/) guide.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Works in German too" icon="translate">
		A dedicated German workflow — source analysis, briefs, drafting, style, and EN→DE
		rewriting. See [German Workflow](/online-writing-skills/skills/german/).
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
