title: Ideation & Drafting
description: Skills that mine ideas, explore angles, and shape raw material into posts, drafts, and articles.
---

These skills take you from "I have a topic" or "I have a pile of notes" to a structured, publishable
piece. They read your [discovery](/online-writing-skills/skills/discovery/) context so ideas and drafts align with your
purpose, buckets, and voice.

## Ideation

### `ideate`

Use when you have a topic but no specific posts to write — or hand over raw material (notes,
transcripts, articles) and want publishable post concepts mined out. Also when you're stuck on what
to write next and need sharp, differentiated angles. Not for expanding a single idea (see
`explore-idea`) or writing the post itself.

### `explore-idea`

Use when you have **one** idea and want to think it through before drafting — especially when it's
still thin on angles, claims, evidence, examples, audience stakes, stories, or business relevance.
A natural follow-on after picking an idea from `ideate`.

## Drafting

Choose the drafting skill by length and destination:

| Skill | Output | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `create-post` | Short-form post, **800–1,200 words** | Social / LinkedIn-length / short blog. Finds the structure hiding in stream-of-consciousness input. |
| `create-draft` | Long-form piece, **2,500–3,000 words** | Full blog post, article, essay, deep dive, feature. |
| `create-medium-post` | Medium-ready article, **800–1,200 words** | Stronger opening hook, compact sectioned flow, title/subtitle treatment, saved directly as Markdown. |

All three read `purpose.md`, `expertise.md`, `buckets.md`, and `tonality.md`, apply the structural
frameworks in `references/post-structure-guide.md`, and match register using
`references/tonality-guide.md`.

### `headlines`

Use when you want headlines generated, workshopped, or iterated — for an existing draft, a topic
brief without a draft yet, or a published piece that needs sharper options. Each headline aims to
communicate the What, the Who, and the Promise, with a curiosity gap. Not for full-post generation or
quality diagnosis.
