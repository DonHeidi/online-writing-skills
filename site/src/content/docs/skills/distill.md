Repurposes one long piece into shorter standalone posts. Part of
[Review](/online-writing-skills/guides/review/).

## When to use it

When you have a long-form piece (article, blog post, essay, draft) and want it broken into shorter
standalone posts in one of two modes:

- **Micros** — ≤280 characters, for X / Bluesky / Threads / Mastodon
- **LinkedIn shorts** — 800–1,200 characters

It produces one mode or both depending on the request. Also use for cross-posting or repurposing a
single long piece into distributable social content.

## When not to use it

- For generating new post ideas from scratch → use [`ideate`](/online-writing-skills/skills/ideate/).
- For shaping raw material into a full post → use [`create-post`](/online-writing-skills/skills/create-post/) (800–1,200 *words*) or [`create-draft`](/online-writing-skills/skills/create-draft/) (2,500–3,000 *words*).

## Example

```text
Distill this piece into five X micros and two LinkedIn shorts.
```
