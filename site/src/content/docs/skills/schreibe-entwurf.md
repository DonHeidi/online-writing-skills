Drafts long-form German prose from a brief. Part of the
[German Workflow](/online-writing-skills/skills/overview/#german-workflow).

## When to use it

When you have a German brief (produced by
[`analysiere-quelle`](/online-writing-skills/skills/analysiere-quelle/), or hand-written) and want a
**long-form German article in the 2,500–3,000 word range** — Blog-Beitrag, Fachartikel, Essay, or
Tiefenbeitrag. Triggers: "schreibe entwurf", "deutscher entwurf", "langform auf deutsch".

## Requires a brief

It needs a German brief as input. If you hand it raw material instead, it will ask whether to run
[`analysiere-quelle`](/online-writing-skills/skills/analysiere-quelle/) first.

:::note[Pipeline gaps]
Short-form German drafting (`schreibe-post`) and German micro-post distillation (`destilliere`) are
referenced but not built yet — see [Known Limitations](/online-writing-skills/guides/limitations/).
:::

## Example

```text
schreibe entwurf — erstelle einen langen deutschen Artikel aus diesem Brief.
```
