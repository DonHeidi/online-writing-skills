Shapes raw material into a long-form piece — full blog post, article, essay, deep dive, or feature.
Part of [Production](/online-writing-skills/guides/production/).

:::note[Entry point for long-form]
This is currently the main entry point for long-form content — start here when you're producing a
full article rather than a short post.
:::

## When to use it

When you have raw material (brain dump, notes, research, bullet points) and want it shaped into a
publishable **long-form piece in the 2,500–3,000 word range**.

## When not to use it

- Short-form (800–1,200 words) → use [`create-post`](/online-writing-skills/skills/create-post/).
- Platform micro-posts → use [`distill`](/online-writing-skills/skills/distill/).
- An existing long-form draft that needs refinement → use [`improve-writing`](/online-writing-skills/skills/improve-writing/).

## Reads

`purpose.md`, `expertise.md`, `buckets.md`, and `tonality.md` — applies your voice and the structural
frameworks across a longer arc.

## Example

```text
Turn this research into a long-form article (use create-draft): <raw material>.
```
