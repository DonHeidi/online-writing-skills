A discovery interview that turns your expertise into a defined content territory. Part of the
[Discovery](/online-writing-skills/guides/discovery/) foundation.

## When to use it

When you have a sense of purpose but don't know which topics you actually own — or you're stuck
between too many possible topics and can't name your genius zone, content territory, or audience-level
categories. Also whenever you want to define, revisit, or refine your content buckets or expert zones.

## When not to use it

- For one-off idea generation → use [`ideate`](/online-writing-skills/skills/ideate/).
- For clarifying motivation and audience → use [`discover-purpose`](/online-writing-skills/skills/discover-purpose/).

## How it works

Five steps: **map expert zones** (list 10–20, breadth first), find the **One Big Key Zone** (your
center of gravity), find the **Genius Zone** (overlap the key zone with 2–3 secondary zones for the
unfair-advantage intersection), **translate to content buckets** (General / Niche / Industry), and
**stress-test** them for alignment, sustainability, specificity, and energy.

## Produces

`expertise.md` (the full zone list) and `buckets.md` (your buckets and topic territory) — both read by
[`ideate`](/online-writing-skills/skills/ideate/) and the drafting skills.

## Example

```text
Run discover-buckets — help me map my expertise into content buckets and find my genius zone.
```
