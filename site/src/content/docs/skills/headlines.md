Produces and sharpens headline options. Part of
[Production](/online-writing-skills/guides/production/).

## When to use it

When you want headlines generated, workshopped, or iterated — for an existing draft, a topic brief
without a draft yet, or a published piece that needs sharper options. Triggers include "give me
headlines", "headline variants", "rework this title", "titles for this post". Also when you show a
draft and ask what to call it, or an existing headline underperforms.

Each variant aims to communicate the **What**, the **Who**, and the **Promise**, with a curiosity gap.

## When not to use it

- For full-post generation → use [`create-post`](/online-writing-skills/skills/create-post/) / [`create-draft`](/online-writing-skills/skills/create-draft/).
- For diagnosing a finished piece's quality → use [`rate`](/online-writing-skills/skills/rate/) / [`diagnose`](/online-writing-skills/skills/diagnose/).

## Example

```text
Give me 10 headline variants for this post: <draft or topic>.
```
