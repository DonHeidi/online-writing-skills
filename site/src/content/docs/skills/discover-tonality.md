A discovery interview that captures your English writing voice. This is the highest-leverage part of
the [Discovery](/online-writing-skills/guides/discovery/) foundation — it keeps every draft sounding
like you.

## When to use it

When your output sounds generic, AI-generated, or misaligned with your natural voice — or when the
register mismatches the piece type (a case study reading as an opinion piece, a descriptive build log
drilling a thesis, instructional content meandering into essay, a curated list turning into an
argument). Also whenever you want to define, revisit, or sharpen your tonality.

## When not to use it

- For defining what to write about → use [`discover-purpose`](/online-writing-skills/skills/discover-purpose/).
- For which topics to own → use [`discover-buckets`](/online-writing-skills/skills/discover-buckets/).
- Writing in German → use [`finde-stil`](/online-writing-skills/skills/finde-stil/) instead.

## How it works

It weaves preference questions, comparisons, and rewrite prompts to extract your values across six
voice dimensions — Commitment, Reasoning Style, Reader Relationship, Emotional Register, Density, and
the agent-specific failure modes to resist — then runs a register check by piece type and synthesises
a profile you confirm. It loads `purpose.md` and `buckets.md` so examples come from your real domain.

## Produces

`tonality.md` — a voice summary, dimension profiles, agent-specific failure modes, register
tendencies, format rules, and 8–10 reference samples from your own rewrites. Every content skill loads
it to match your voice and check output against your anti-patterns.

## Example

```text
Run discover-tonality — build my voice profile from examples so drafts sound like me.
```
