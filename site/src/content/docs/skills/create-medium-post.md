Drafts a Medium-ready article with a stronger opening hook, compact sectioned flow, and title/subtitle
treatment, saved directly as a Markdown file. Part of
[Production](/online-writing-skills/guides/production/).

## When to use it

When you have raw material, notes, a brain dump, outline, transcript, or loose ideas and want a
**Medium-ready 800–1,200 word article or essay** saved as Markdown — with a stronger opening hook,
compact sectioned flow, reader retention, title/subtitle treatment, and publication notes when needed.

## When not to use it

- Generic 800–1,200 word short-form posts → use [`create-post`](/online-writing-skills/skills/create-post/).
- Generic 2,500–3,000 word blog drafts → use [`create-draft`](/online-writing-skills/skills/create-draft/).

## Reads

`purpose.md`, `expertise.md`, `buckets.md`, and `tonality.md`.

## Example

```text
Use create-medium-post to turn these notes into a Medium-ready article.
```
