Builds a German voice profile. The German counterpart to
[`discover-tonality`](/online-writing-skills/skills/discover-tonality/); see the
[German Workflow](/online-writing-skills/skills/overview/#german-workflow) overview.

## When to use it

When you want to define, revisit, or sharpen your **German** writing voice — or when German content
skills consistently produce output that doesn't sound like your German. Triggers: "finde meinen stil",
"deutsche tonalität", "deutscher schreibstil".

## Independent of discover-tonality

This skill does **not** read `tonality.md`. It produces `stil.md` from scratch, in German.

## Produces

`stil.md` — your German voice profile, read by the German content skills.

## Example

```text
finde meinen stil — hilf mir, meinen deutschen Schreibstil zu definieren.
```
