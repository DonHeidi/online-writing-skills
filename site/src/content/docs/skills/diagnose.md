---
title: diagnose
description: Flag structural issues in a piece against the post-structure playbook — without rewriting.
---

Flags structural problems and suggests fixes. It does **not** rewrite or score — run it first in
[Review](/online-writing-skills/guides/review/) to know where to focus.

## When to use it

When you have a written piece (yours or someone else's) and want structural issues flagged against the
post-structure playbook — slow intros, blurring main points, low Rate of Revelation, forced
conclusions, headline/intro mismatch, rhythm problems. Typically run after a draft is produced, or
standalone ("what's wrong with this?" / "where is this weak?").

## When not to use it

- For overall quality scoring → use [`rate`](/online-writing-skills/skills/rate/).
- For rewriting → use [`improve-writing`](/online-writing-skills/skills/improve-writing/).

:::note[Guidance, not ground truth]
Diagnose reflects the framework's judgment, not an objective measure. Use it to focus attention. See
[Known Limitations](/online-writing-skills/guides/limitations/).
:::

## Example

```text
Diagnose this draft and tell me why it doesn't land yet.
```
