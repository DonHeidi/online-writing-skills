Turns a topic — or a pile of raw material — into publishable post concepts. Part of
[Production](/online-writing-skills/guides/production/).

## When to use it

When you have a topic but no specific posts to write about it, or you hand over raw material (notes,
transcripts, articles) and want publishable post concepts mined out. Also when you're stuck on what to
write next and need sharp, differentiated angles.

## When not to use it

- For expanding a single idea into drafting material → use [`explore-idea`](/online-writing-skills/skills/explore-idea/).
- For writing the post itself → use [`create-post`](/online-writing-skills/skills/create-post/) or [`create-draft`](/online-writing-skills/skills/create-draft/).

## Reads

`purpose.md`, `expertise.md`, and `buckets.md` — so concepts align with your purpose and sit inside
your content territory.

## Example

```text
Use my online-writing context to ideate post concepts about <topic>.
```
