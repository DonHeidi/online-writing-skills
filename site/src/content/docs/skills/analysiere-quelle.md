Extracts a German brief from raw material so a German long-form piece can be drafted from it. Part of
the [German Workflow](/online-writing-skills/skills/overview/#german-workflow).

## When to use it

When you have raw material — an English article, a German article, a brain dump in either language, a
transcript, mixed notes — and want a **German brief** extracted from it as the planning artefact for a
German article. Triggers: "analysiere quelle", "deutsche zusammenfassung", "brief auf deutsch",
"extrahiere kern".

## Extraction, not translation

The brief is in German, but it is **not a translation** — it is an extraction of the core. The brief
is what [`schreibe-entwurf`](/online-writing-skills/skills/schreibe-entwurf/) reads.

## Example

```text
analysiere quelle — erstelle mir einen deutschen Brief aus diesem Material.
```
