Develops a single idea until it has enough substance to draft well. Part of
[Production](/online-writing-skills/guides/production/).

:::caution[Gotcha]
It can drift away from your original thesis as it explores — keep an eye on the through-line and pull
it back if the exploration wanders off the point you actually want to make.
:::

## When to use it

When you have one writing idea and want to think it through before drafting — especially when it's
still thin on angles, claims, evidence, examples, audience stakes, stories, contradictions, or
business relevance. A natural next step after picking an idea from
[`ideate`](/online-writing-skills/skills/ideate/).

## When not to use it

- For generating many separate ideas → use [`ideate`](/online-writing-skills/skills/ideate/).
- For turning finished material into a structured draft → use [`create-post`](/online-writing-skills/skills/create-post/) or [`create-draft`](/online-writing-skills/skills/create-draft/).

## Example

```text
Let's explore this idea before I draft it: <idea>.
```
