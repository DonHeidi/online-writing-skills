---
title: Refine & Repurpose
description: Improve, diagnose, rate, abstract, repurpose, and illustrate finished pieces.
---

Once a draft exists, these skills make it stronger and stretch it further. The first three are
complementary: `improve-writing` changes the text, `diagnose` flags issues without rewriting, and
`rate` benchmarks quality.

## Refine

### `improve-writing`

Use when an existing draft already has structure — headers, partial sections, framework annotations,
a clear direction — and wants to be refined, tightened, restructured, or finished. Signals: the draft
feels flat or rough, sections trail off, you can't name what's wrong but know it isn't ready. Not for
raw unstructured input (see [`create-post` / `create-draft`](/online-writing-skills/skills/ideation-drafting/)).

### `diagnose`

Use when you want structural issues flagged against the post-structure playbook — slow intros,
blurring main points, low Rate of Revelation, forced conclusions, headline/intro mismatch, rhythm
problems. Typically run after a draft is produced, or standalone ("what's wrong with this?").
**Diagnose only flags and suggests** — it does not rewrite (see `improve-writing`) or score (see
`rate`).

### `rate`

Use when you need an objective quality assessment — your own draft before publishing, someone else's
article before committing time, or two versions compared. Produces a **VPM (value per minute)** read
and scores across quality dimensions. Signals: "is this worth my time", "is this ready to ship", "how
does this score". Rate only evaluates; it does not rewrite.

## Repurpose

### `tldr`

Use when you have a long article (typically 2,500–3,000 words) and want a single-paragraph abstract
(~100–150 words) for the top of the piece — a hybrid academic abstract / synopsis in your voice that
lets a reader decide whether to commit.

### `distill`

Use when you have a long-form piece and want it broken into shorter standalone posts, in one of two
modes: **Micros** (≤280 chars, for X / Bluesky / Threads / Mastodon) or **LinkedIn shorts**
(800–1,200 chars). Produces one or both depending on the request — ideal for cross-posting and
repurposing.

## Illustrate

### `illustrate`

Use when a publish-ready piece is missing imagery — banner, header, hero image, section illustration,
or social share graphic. Produces **Midjourney prompts** and can be followed by an image-flow
automation for submission and asset wiring. Not for non-Midjourney generators, stock-photo search, or
image editing. If the piece isn't written yet, draft it first.
