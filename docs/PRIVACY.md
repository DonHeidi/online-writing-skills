# Privacy Policy

Online Writing is a local Codex plugin that packages writing skills, reference material, scripts, and assets.

The plugin does not include an external service, hosted backend, telemetry collector, analytics SDK, database, or remote API integration. It does not independently collect, store, sell, or transmit personal data.

When you use the plugin, Codex may read the writing project files, drafts, notes, configuration files, and source material that you authorize it to access in your local workspace. That data is handled by Codex and OpenAI according to the terms, privacy policy, workspace settings, and approval controls that apply to your Codex environment.

The plugin may help create or update local files such as `.online-writing/purpose.md`, `.online-writing/buckets.md`, `.online-writing/tonality.md`, `.online-writing/stil.md`, `.online-writing/settings.md`, article drafts, briefs, diagnostics, ratings, or generated prompts. Those files stay in the workspace unless you or Codex, with your authorization, move or publish them elsewhere.

The `illustrate` skill creates prompts for Midjourney. It does not submit prompts to Midjourney by itself. If you copy generated prompts into Midjourney or another external service, that service's own terms and privacy policy apply.

Questions or concerns can be opened as issues at:

https://github.com/DonHeidi/online-writing-skills/issues
